# Advancement track visual rework (#85)

The owner reviewed the four tracks in the component gallery and approved the final symbolic layouts on 2026-10-29. This document records the decisions embodied in the components.

- Keep the four tracks' pointed tile geometry, costs, selection states and progress lanes. Center content between the actual angled edges. First tiles have a flat left edge and their content is optically centered like Exploration's first tile.
- Put the tile name at top left and its number at top right. Reserve the bottom for the landmark icon and name, aligned the same way on all tracks. Landmark spaces keep their golden emphasis without a gold tile border.
- Show the primary effect with icons centered vertically when there is no optional effect. When there is one, use the upper half for the main effect and the lower half for a compact optional band. The band's top fades from the track accent colour to transparency. The tile outline draws over it.
- Use individual unfilled hexes for territory tiles. A space tile has stars; exploration adds a thick downward arrow. Use the approved outpost flag, science die, capital city, victory cup and card-back art. Resource and building art stays at its native size where possible.
- Operators are gold and spaced on both sides: plus for combined effects, × for multiplication, and / for alternatives. Optional price and reward are separated by a bold colon with spaces around it. Text-only conditions remain legible and do not masquerade as icon groups.
- Science progress spaces put the direction arrow beside a vertical column of eligible tracks. Quantum Physics repeats the group. Alien Biology shows four dice in a column, then ×, /, and the 5 VP cup on one line.
- Technology's multi-part benefits have explicit rows and operators. AI Singularity shows a resource column, a plus, and a restart arrow above a larger track column. Card art keeps the technology and tapestry proportions and the civilization symbol uses its approved vector trace.
- The victory cup is one consistent size per tile scale across all tracks. Compact responsive layouts preserve icon order and prevent the Mechs dice from wrapping.

The gallery's TrackBoard, TrackSpace, ExplorationFace and CardSymbol sections are the visual reference. The game uses the same components, so later changes to tile presentation should be made there.
