Income tab · lift a building to see the space under it (#142)

Point at any building on a dark space, or press Tab to reach one with the keyboard. The building lifts off the mat, its dark resting tile goes with it, and the printed space underneath shows its name and reward exactly as an exposed space does. Move away and it settles back. Nothing else on the mat moves.

A · Straight up

The building rises straight up until it floats just above its space, 15% larger, with the shadow a dragged piece casts (--shadow-float) and a soft shadow on the mat along the space's top edge. It hangs over the row's heading, never over another space.

B · Up and aside

The building rises, drifts a little to the left and tips 10°, as if picked up by one corner, with the same shadows. It feels more handled, and still clears the printed name and reward.

  • Both options keep every space its size, so the mat never shifts; the dark tile fades with the same timing as the building, and the row-coloured tint on hover goes, since the revealed space says more.
  • Reduced motion: the building and its tile fade where they stand, without moving. The game's motion tokens are 0 ms under reduced motion, so in the game this fade is instant.
  • Keyboard: each covered space takes the focus (gold outline) and lifts its building while it holds it.
