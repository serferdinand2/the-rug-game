Proposed words (#148)

Each prompt from a tapestry card or a civilization names the card or civilization, says what is chosen, and each answer says what it does. Where the card says "you and your neighbours", the neighbour's own prompt names whose card it is. Where a prompt comes from many places at once, such as a plain "Choose a resource", the line saying where it comes from stays #60's (Lazar's).
