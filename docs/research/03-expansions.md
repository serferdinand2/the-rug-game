# Tapestry — Expansions & Promos Catalogue (rules-engine reference)

> **Decisions:** every **[UNVERIFIED]** item and open question in this file is decided in the README's [Rules conventions](../../README.md#rules-conventions) (R1–R110). Follow those decisions, not the working assumptions here.

Scope: every official expansion, accessory and promo for Stonemaier Games' *Tapestry* (2019) except the base game itself (covered by other research files). Compiled 2026-10-20 from the official rulebooks (incl. the 32-page comprehensive F&F rulebook and the 20-page comprehensive Automa rulebook), Stonemaier product/FAQ pages, Jamey Stegmaier's BGG design diaries, official rulings on BGG (Jamey Stegmaier, Stonemaier's Joe Aubrey, co-designer Mike Young), photos of the components, and the community BGG wiki (cross-checked).

Conventions
- Effects are paraphrased; every number and condition is kept exact. **[UNVERIFIED]** marks anything not confirmed by a primary source or a legible component image.
- Resource names: coin, worker, food, culture; "any" = any 1 resource of your choice. Income buildings: market (yellow; gained mainly from the technology track), house (gray; science), farm (brown; exploration), armory (red; military). "Invent" = gain a tech card into your bottom row. "Research" = roll the science die and advance on the rolled track (with or without benefit as stated). "Explore" = place a territory tile. WP / TE / CONT / R = WHEN PLAYED / THIS ERA / CONTINUOUS / response (discard-from-hand) tapestry cards.
- Footprints: rows top→bottom separated by `/`, `X` = occupied square, `.` = empty.
- Rules tagged PP / AA / FF in the comprehensive rulebook apply only when that expansion is in play; several "rule amendments" introduced by expansions apply to every game (see §7).

### 1.2 Rule changes and new mechanisms

**Initial setup (one-time integration)**
1. Shuffle the 10 civ mats and 15 tapestry cards into the base components.
2. Put all (base) territory tiles in the exploration bag; draw territory tiles from the bag from now on (no new territory tiles in P&P).
3. Remove the misprinted base-game space tile and shuffle the corrected replacement tile plus the 4 new tiles into the space-tile stack (base 15 + 4 = 19 space tiles).
4. Optional: place the 12 landmark tokens on their landmark spaces of the advancement tracks; remove each when that landmark is taken.

**Landmark cards (new card type — "personal achievements")**
- Timing in setup: after the first player is chosen (end of normal setup), the player sitting to the right of the first player (= the player going last) draws landmark cards equal to the number of players (players + 1 if both P&P and A&A landmark cards are in the pool), keeps 1, passes the rest **counter-clockwise**; each player in turn keeps 1. Each player places the card left of their civ mat and its miniature on/near it. Any leftover cards are shuffled into a face-down landmark deck (used by Urban Planners, Infrastructure Upgrade, etc.).
- At the **end of one of your turns** (advance or income turn), if you meet your card's goal, you **must** gain that landmark and place it in your capital city (mandatory and immediate; cannot be delayed — Joe Aubrey, BGG). It cannot be met on another player's turn or after your final income turn.
- Each player can gain only the landmark of the one card they drafted (Mike Young/Joe Aubrey ruling) — meeting other cards' conditions does nothing.
- Solo: you draw 2 landmark cards, keep 1; the other card's landmark goes in the landmark box of the Automa's income mat (A&A adds 2 more for Automa difficulty 3-4 when the arts track is used — see A&A Automa).
- 2-player Shadow Empire variant: the Shadow Empire is not a player for landmark-card drafting.

**Response ("trap-like") cards**
- 5 new tapestry cards can be discarded from hand in response to an opponent's conquer attempt or trap, or played normally as a tapestry card (each shows an "If played as a tapestry, gain …" WHEN PLAYED line).
- Terminology from the comprehensive rulebook: "response cards" = any card discarded in response to another player's action; "trap cards" = only the base cards actually titled **Trap**. Anything that reacts to "a trap" (Double Cross, Stolen Plans, Pillage & Plunder's "traps cannot be played") ignores other response cards (Joe Aubrey, confirmed 2024).
- Trap-response cards (Double Cross, Stolen Plans) may only answer a Trap; nothing can answer a trap-response; once a trap and a trap-response have been played, no further cards can be played for that conquer action. Only **one** response card per defender per conquer attempt (Surrender + Surprise Party together is illegal). Retreat cannot follow a cancelled trap (the conquer is over).
- Surprise Party is not a Trap: the attacker cannot answer it with Stolen Plans/Double Cross, and since it topples rather than conquers, it cannot be answered with a Trap (Jamey, Aug 2024).
- If any response card (e.g., Surprise Party; F&F Gremlins) successfully topples your outpost when you conquer the middle island, you do **not** gain the middle-island achievement.
- No response cards may be played after your 5th income turn unless your civ explicitly allows it.

**Other rules introduced by P&P (apply to all games)**
- **New tiebreaker** (replaces the original): the player with the tallest landmark miniature in their capital city wins. Jamey's height notes: Fusion Reactor (base) is the tallest of all; Skyscraper is slightly taller than A&A's Holocenter; Digital Studio is taller than TV Station "by a hair". No official height ranking table exists **[UNVERIFIED full ordering]**.
- If the tapestry deck runs out, shuffle the discard pile to form a new deck; if there are no discards, nobody can draw tapestry cards.
- A space tile (or other effect) that moves you several spaces at once counts as a single "advance" (relevant for the "+5 VP whenever you advance on track X" tiles).
- The 5 P&P response cards are **kept** in solo games (they are not removed with the traps).

### 1.7 Automa / Shadow Empire (P&P)

Setup and rule changes (now in the comprehensive F&F Automa rulebook, tagged PP):
- Remove the **Advisors** and **Spies** civs (in addition to the base removals). In the comprehensive list Advisors is marked "may be used but will unfairly benefit/hurt you".
- Give the Automa a random civ from **all** Automa civs; if its favourite track is shown as the science-die icon, roll the die to set it.
- Landmark cards: you gain 2 and keep 1; the other's landmark goes in the landmark box of the Automa income mat.
- Keep all P&P response cards in the deck. When the Automa draws (when you conquer it): **Double Cross, Stolen Plans, Surrender** act as ordinary non-trap cards (keep drawing); **Retreat, Surprise Party** work as written if the Automa meets the condition (Retreat: hex tiebreaker picks the destination), otherwise discard and keep drawing.
- Favourite tracks are now marked with **2 player tokens moved together** instead of an outpost (frees an outpost for conquering) — also adopted for the base game.
- Tiebreaking without a card: if the Automa needs a tiebreaker with no decision card face up, draw one from the progress deck (comprehensive rules: shuffle it back after use).
- Historians: Automa and Shadow Empire are separate players (landmarks gained by the Shadow Empire don't trigger tokens held only by the Automa).

### 1.8 Errata, FAQ and rulings specific to P&P

- **Misprint fix:** the replacement space tile (see §1.5). First base printings had civ-mat backs upside down; P&P corrected this (no gameplay effect). Advisors text typo "instead of them drawing a from the deck" (missing "card").
- Infiltrators: the 3 tokens for the extra civ must be in the **same** opponent capital territory. Infiltrators + Riverfolk (draw 3 → if you take Riverfolk you may discard it and "gain another"): official ruling **C** — the "draw 3 keep 1" triggers again (Joe Aubrey, 2021).
- Recyclers vs Inventors: the Inventors may place a cube on a Recyclers tech; only the Recyclers themselves can recycle top→bottom and only they gain the 5 VP; an Inventors cube follows the card back to the bottom row; any number of Inventors cubes may share a card.
- Spies + Espionage: Spies (and Heralds) may copy Espionage and through it copy a THIS ERA card, which lasts only until your next income turn.
- After your final income turn you can gain VP from passive civ abilities but cannot take actions that need input (e.g., Advisors giving cards, playing response cards).
- Utilitarians' Lighthouse may be used before or after the advance on that turn.
- Treasure Hunters: each treasure (terrain) usable once per game; the terrain is that of the empty adjacent hex being claimed; "research die" = science die.
- Stolen Plans also makes the trapper lose a random tapestry card (they lose 2 cards total and are toppled) — Mike Young.
- Historians (base) are not triggered by Shadow Empire landmarks if only the Automa holds their token.

### 2.2 Rule changes and new mechanisms

**Setup additions** (comprehensive rulebook order):
1. *Global step 2 (AA):* place the arts track beside the board; shuffle the 20 masterpiece cards face down next to it and reveal 3 face up.
2. *Global step 7:* optional landmark tokens (12 from P&P + 3 arts).
3. *Global step 8:* use the d20 science die instead of the d12 (optional variant: "you may choose to roll either science die").
4. *Player step A (AA/FF):* in addition to the basic capital city mat(s) (1 mat at 4-5 players; a random pre-paired set of 2 at 1-3 players), each player draws 1 random **advanced** capital city mat. Place your 2 starting outposts on the territory matching the number(s) of the **basic** mat(s) drawn; then keep 1 capital city mat (basic or advanced) and 1 of your 2 civs simultaneously (choose them together, since some advanced cities suit some civs). Starting territory is not changed by keeping the advanced mat. Recommended: new players keep a basic mat. Riverfolk may not choose Cloud City (F&F adjustment / revised civ).
5. *Player step D:* each player keeps a set of 4 inspiration tiles beside (not on) their mats.
6. *Player step E:* each player also puts 1 player token on the start space of the arts track.
7. *Landmark cards:* drafted as in P&P; if both P&P and A&A landmark cards are owned, deal players **+1** cards, so the last drafter chooses from 2; leftovers form the landmark deck.

**Income turn change:** new step **"1. Activate masterpiece(s), then civilization(s)"** — at the very beginning of income turns 2-5 ("step 0"; you cannot own one before income turn 2), gain the benefits of all your masterpiece cards in any order, before civ abilities. Masterpieces gained during this step may be activated immediately; the same masterpiece may be activated more than once in a phase if an effect allows it; activation is mandatory, except that masterpieces requiring a payment (Costuming, Mime) are declined by not paying; Art Deco and Mime are single-use (flip face down). Masterpieces are also triggered by the Broadcast benefit.

**Rules that A&A amended for every game (with or without the expansion):**
- You may not activate the same space of an advancement track more than once during your turn (closes repeat-loops, e.g., Academic Research/Lithium-Ion; also applies to the arts track).
- You may not respond to other players (e.g., response cards) after your final income turn; you can still passively gain VP from civ abilities.
- Tiebreaker = tallest landmark in your capital city (as in P&P).
- With other landmark cards, deal players + 1 landmark cards.
- **Dark Ages** (base card) with the arts track: after regressing on 3 different tracks you choose which one of the 2 remaining tracks advances 3 times.
- **Alchemists** (base civ) with the d20: if you roll the arts icon you may roll again or stop; only the original 4 track icons count for the Alchemists. No arts-related change for Leaders or Entertainers; the base science-track spaces that let you advance on "one of the other tracks" keep their printed tracks (arts not added).
- Dystopia (base) can take any of the 3 arts-track landmarks (they count as part of the landmark board).

**Landmark-prerequisite tech cards (AA):** a tech card showing the landmark-prerequisite icon needs a landmark **on the card itself** to go to the top row. While no landmark is on it, whenever you gain any landmark you may place it on that card (size irrelevant) instead of your capital, which instantly upgrades the card once (you resolve that upgrade — and anything it triggers — before continuing with the benefit that gave you the landmark). The landmark stays there all game (discarded if the tech card is discarded). One landmark satisfies only the card it sits on. Normal upgrades can still move the card from the bottom to the middle row. Duct Tape's "ignore prerequisites" upgrades ignore this requirement.

**New icons:** create (gain masterpiece), refresh-then-create, broadcast (gain the benefit of 1 of your masterpieces), inspire (place an inspiration tile), "upgrade a tech card that may not be upgraded again this turn", "perform 2 upgrades ignoring prerequisites", "1 VP per completed column", "1 VP per completed row" (these are **not** "scoring your capital city", so Swamp/Urban-Planners-type triggers don't fire), "conquer an adjacent **empty** territory", "any income building", "1 VP per income building", "1 VP per arts-track space advanced", "you and your neighbours".

**Tapestry-card innovations:** CONTINUOUS cards (effect lasts until the card is covered or the game ends; cannot be copied by Espionage/Heralds etc.); building-plot cards (Commercial Center, Monuments, Star Chamber, Trade Routes): income buildings and landmarks may be placed on these cards instead of your capital city, landmarks may overhang the card's grid; buildings on such cards count as yours when counting buildings of a type ("score as if in your capital city", e.g., Steel's "1 VP per armory"), but they do not count as *in your capital city* for rows/columns/districts, Monarchy, Architects etc.; if the card is ever covered by another tapestry card, all buildings/landmarks on it are discarded and no longer count.

**Arts-track cost symbols:** tier I = any 1 resource; tier II = 2 *different* resources; tier III = any 3 resources; tier IV = 2 resources of the *same* type (board icon "any = any"; the A&A FAQ explains only the tier II "≠" symbol, and a BGG question describing tier IV as "2 same resources" went uncorrected by the officials who answered it).

**Landmark tech cards** (5: Astrolabe, Automation, Calculus, Microphone, War College):
- **Card text:** "If there is no landmark on this card, whenever you gain a landmark, you may place it on this card to immediately upgrade [card]. The prerequisite for upgrading to the top row is that there is a landmark on this card."
- **Landmark size:** irrelevant. The landmark stays for the rest of the game and is discarded with the card.
- **One card each:** a landmark on one such card does not satisfy another [RB-AA p3].
- **Upgrade paths:** both are legal. A landmark on a bottom-row card moves it to the middle row; a later normal upgrade then reaches the top row. A corrected Oil Paint replacement card ships in Fantasies & Futures, and the FF rulebook p24 shows the icon.

### FF.2 New rules and mechanisms (exact procedures)

Source unless noted: comprehensive rulebook FF_Rulebook_r10-1 [RB-FF], pages given as pNN. Rules marked FF in that rulebook apply only when F&F is in play.

#### FF.2.1 Charms (charm bonuses) [RB-FF p6-7]

- A "charmed" tapestry card has a charm tag on its left edge, its right edge, or both. Each tag shows a bonus (sometimes "X OR Y").
- **Left charm**: gained when you play this card to the right of another tapestry card.
  - The card to the left may be face down. It may or may not have a charm. You may be placing this card on top of other tapestries.
  - **Maker of Fire is not a card.** A card played in the era-2 slot next to Maker of Fire does not get its left charm (Jamey Stegmaier, BGG thread 3051013 and 3032934). Official F&F FAQ: "Can I gain a left-side charm on a tapestry card if Maker of Fire is directly to the left of it? Nope!"
  - A card played on top of Maker of Fire (e.g. by Heralds) gets its left charm, because era 1's printed Maker of Fire is then covered by a real card. Rulebook p7 says "It does not matter if that card to the left is face down or on Maker of Fire." Jamey confirmed in 3032934 that covering Maker of Fire with a card triggers charms.
- **Right charm**: gained the first time you place a tapestry card to the right of this card.
  - The new card to the right may be face down. It may or may not have a charm.
  - The trigger is "the first time": a later card placed on top of the right-hand stack does not re-trigger it.
  - If the charmed card itself is later covered (overplay), its right charm can still trigger when you play the next era's card. [INFERRED from "Charmed tapestry cards are the only cards that may have effects after they are covered" p7, and "Nor does it matter if this card is being placed on top of other tapestries" for left charms. Engine note: track the right-charm-triggered flag per card instance, not per slot. **[UNVERIFIED]** for the covered-card case.]
- **Order**: you may resolve the charm bonus(es) and the card's WHEN PLAYED ability in any order.
- **Mandatory**: charm benefits are mandatory if possible, including an "advance without benefit" charm (e.g. Lost Artifacts' technology advance).
  - Joe Aubrey: "Tapestry benefits are required (if possible), unless otherwise noted." (BGG 3032934, 2023-03-01)
  - Mike Young: "Unfortunately they are not optional." (BGG 3066347, 2023-04-15)
- **Not copied**: other cards (e.g. Espionage) and other civilizations (e.g. Heralds) copy only a tapestry's ability, never its charm bonuses [p7].
  - Copying an uncovered card's WHEN PLAYED ability that reserves an item is allowed. Example: Espionage copying Microbial Magic reserves a farm [p7].
- **Continuous abilities** (A&A and F&F) apply from when played until the end of the game. They cannot be copied (e.g. by Espionage) [p7].
- **Merfolk**: when the Merfolk play a card onto the era-4 stack after income turn 5, they gain its left-hand charm bonus as usual (Merfolk mat).
- **Card counting**: "tapestry card charm currently visible on your income mat" (Wizards' Guild) counts individual charm tags on uncovered cards. A card with 2 charms counts 2, and Wizards' Guild counts its own charms (Mike Young, BGG 3266577 article 43969090, 2024-03-16). Covered cards are not visible (Mike Young, BGG 3053736).
- **Which cards are charmed**: Mike Young says "we deliberately made every Tapestry card in the 3rd expansion a charmed card" (BGG 3222793, 2024-01-10). The design diary says only "as many of the cards … had charms". See the card list (FF.4) for the actual charms.

#### FF.2.2 Income turn 5 reserved items ("[5]" mechanism) [RB-FF p4, p7, p15, p16, p28, p30]

- **Setup step C (new)**: place an income mat overlay over the right edge of your income mat. Tuck the overlay's right side beneath your capital city mat.
- Some tapestry cards' WHEN PLAYED abilities tell you to place an item at the right edge of your income mat (onto the overlay). That item is **reserved**. Items can be:
  - your next income building of a type (Microbial Magic: farm; Phantasmal Paladins: armory);
  - a card (e.g. a tech card), placed face up;
  - a tile (e.g. a space tile), placed face up;
  - a reminder (a player token / "[5]" effect such as VP or an advance). See the card list for each.
- **Effect of moving a building**: moving the next building of a type onto the overlay uncovers its income-mat space immediately. That space produces income or VP from then on. You still may not place the building into your capital until income turn 5.
- **Use timing**: reserved items are used during income turn 5 at the moment you would ordinarily play a tapestry card. The income-turn order is: (1) masterpieces, then civilization abilities; (2) use reserved items, in place of the tapestry step; (3) upgrade a tech card and score VP; (4) no income on IT5.
- Reserved items **cannot be used before income turn 5** by other means. Example: you can't take the reserved farm with the Tunnels advancement.
- Reserved buildings are **not** "in your capital city" before income turn 5. Example: they don't count for the arts track's Virtual Reality Holocenter (Joe Aubrey, BGG 3132563, 2023-08-08). They do count for the card's own text, e.g. Microbial Magic: "Include this building when scoring farms."
- On income turn 5 a reserved income building may be placed anywhere that accepts income buildings, not only the capital mat, e.g. Commercial Center (Mike Young, BGG 3774818, 2026-09-28/10-01).
- Reserved items **are still used if the reserving card has been covered**. "Charmed tapestry cards are the only cards that may have effects after they are covered."
  - Mike Young: reminder tokens for VP or advances placed on the [5] overlay also still pay out after their card is covered (BGG 3147487, 2023-09-04).
- **Card played on or after IT5**:
  - New general rule, for all games: if you ever get to play a tapestry card during or after income turn 5, play it on top of your era-4 card [p15, p28].
  - With F&F: if that card reserves an item, use the item immediately [p15]. FAQ p30: "If it's era 5, and I play a tapestry card that reserves an item, may I use that item immediately? Yes."
- **Ending the game** [p15]: during your final income turn you gain civilization benefits, any reserved items, 1 upgrade, and VP from your income mat.
  - Jamey: income turn 5 is still your final turn. "The types of things reserved to gain during that turn (in Fantasies & Futures) are for scoring, not resources." (BGG 3052331)
  - Mike Young: if a masterpiece or tech card gives you a resource on IT5 you may collect it (BGG 3208351). It is normally useless, except for the Elder Ones and Merfolk, who keep playing.
- **THIS ERA cards around IT5** (Mike Young, BGG 3037126, 2023-02-27):
  - A THIS ERA card played in era 4 is **not** in effect during income turn 5 (Elder Ones excepted).
  - A THIS ERA card you manage to play **during** income turn 5 (e.g. via a space tile or Nuclear Bomb) is in effect for the rest of income turn 5.
  - F&F adds no normal "play a tapestry" step to IT5. The designers told the BGA implementer that for the Elder Ones the era-4 card stays active through IT5 [DQ 7].
- **Overplay after era 4**: playing a card on top of an existing card after era 4 goes on the era-4 card. Without F&F, Mike Young says the old rule applies: no card is played after era 4 (BGG 3037126, 2024-09-03).
  - Mike Young: this does NOT let you overplay onto an empty slot in earlier eras. An overplay with no card in the current era's slot is still void (BGG 3193257).
  - Because era 4 ends at the start of income turn 5, an "on top of your current tapestry" effect during income turn 5 itself (e.g. Aliens' space tile, Printing Press upgraded on IT5) lands on the era-4 card.

#### FF.2.3 Tech-card prerequisite "charm / income turn 5" and single-use techs [RB-FF p11, p17, p24-26]

- **Prerequisite icon**: a small tapestry-card icon with a charm, then "/", then a yellow "5". Appears on all 12 F&F techs.
- **Rule**: such a tech may be upgraded to the top row only if you have **ever** played a charmed tapestry card onto your income mat, or you are on or after your income turn 5.
  - A charmed card still satisfies this after it is covered.
  - One charmed card satisfies the prerequisite for any number of techs.
  - **Neighbors are ignored**. These cards have no "you or your neighbors" icon.
  - **[UNVERIFIED]**: does a charmed card played by other means count, e.g. on top of Maker of Fire by Heralds, or by Merfolk after IT5? The text "played a charmed tapestry card onto your income mat" suggests yes.
- **Single-use ("FLIP THIS CARD (SINGLE USE)")**: the square benefit may be gained only once.
  - When you gain it, flip the tech face down. A flipped card still counts as a tech card for scoring (e.g. "1 VP per tech card").
  - **Other players may not** upgrade your single-use techs to the top row or use your flipped techs (e.g. via civilization or tapestry abilities) [p11].
  - Inventors FAQ [p29]: if an opponent upgrades a card holding an Inventors token to the top row, the opponent gets the square benefit, then the Inventors take the card into their bottom row with all tokens. If the stolen card is single-use, it must be flipped and may not be upgraded further.
- 8 of the 12 F&F techs are single-use. Golem Minions, Pyrokinesis, Super Speed and Transmogrification are not (see FF.5).

#### FF.2.5 Setup changes [RB-FF p4-5]

- **Capital city drafting**:
  - 4-5 players: draw 1 basic capital city mat.
  - 1-3 players: draw a random pair of basic mats (paired by the numbers on the board).
  - With AA or FF: also draw a random advanced capital city mat.
  - Place your 2 starting outposts on the territory matching the basic mat number(s) you drew. Then keep 1 capital city mat and 1 of 2 civilization mats; discard the rest.
  - The starting territory is therefore set by the basic mat drawn, even if you keep the advanced mat.
  - Basic and advanced capital mats are never shuffled together. The rulebook recommends new players keep a basic mat.
  - Discarded civilizations are reshuffled.
- **Income mat overlay** (step C): see FF.2.2.
- **Compatibility**: all F&F components shuffle into any combination of base and expansions. If you don't own A&A or choose not to use it, you need not remove anything. When you draw a tapestry or tech card containing the arts symbol you may immediately discard it and draw another [p4].
  - Jamey: every F&F card that references A&A also offers a non-A&A option (BGG 3032934, 3033612).
  - Cards with arts options include Object Cloning (arts OR technology), Perfect Memory (science OR arts), and Good Omens per Chris Scaffidi's blog.

#### FF.2.6 Optional variants added or extended by F&F [RB-FF p16]

- You may require all players to draw civilizations from the same expansion (PP/AA/FF).
- **Income turn 1**: draw 2 tapestries instead of 1, keep 1. The Psionics draw 3, keep 1, discard 2.
- **Civilization draws**: whenever drawing a civilization, draw 2, keep 1, discard the other. The Psionics draw 3, keep 1, discard 2.
- Other variants (unchanged from earlier products):
  - removing too-strong cards;
  - A&A science die choice;
  - neoprene Nanotechnology bonus = discard 2 tech cards;
  - start in the center of the closest island.

#### FF.2.7 Other general rule clarifications printed in the F&F comprehensive rulebook (apply to all games)

- **Tapestry play during step 1 of an income turn** (via a masterpiece or civilization ability, e.g. upgrading Printing Press): the new era has started, so play onto the new era's slot. On income turn 5, play onto the era-4 stack [p28].
- **Response cards** [p12]:
  - Any response card discarded to topple the attacker (FF Gremlins, PP Surprise Party) also denies the middle-island achievement, like a trap.
  - Response cards may not be played after your income turn 5 unless your civilization mat allows it. The Merfolk mat does allow it.
  - Each response card can alternatively be played as a regular tapestry card.
- **Optional vs mandatory**: all bonuses are optional; benefits and landmarks are mandatory [p15].
- **Tiebreak**: the tallest landmark in your capital city wins ties [p15].
  - City in a Bottle owners have no landmarks in the city, so they always lose ties (Mike Young, BGG 3053734).

  - The first printing of Faefolk omitted "2-5". Chris Scaffidi and Mike Young confirmed IT2-5 (BGG 3048419, 3053736), and the r4 file prints "At the start of income turns 2-5".

#### Artificers — "pull innovations from a future into the present"

- **Setup**: none.
- **Start of income turns 2-4**: select 1 income track (e.g. farms). Then choose one:
  - **(a) Set aside** the leftmost building on that track. It goes out of play, not into your capital city.
  - **(b) Slide**: if there is at least one empty space to its left, slide that leftmost building left to cover the leftward space. This reveals a different space to its right.
  - This changes which income-track spaces are exposed, and so your income and VP. Continue to take buildings from left to right during play.
- **Start of income turn 5**: choose one:
  - score 1 VP per income building in your capital city; or
  - select 1 income track and slide all buildings on it as far leftward as possible.
- **Rulings**:
  - The slide moves the building exactly **1 space** left ("the leftward space"). Chris Scaffidi, BGG 3035872.
  - The spaces exposed at game start are legal slide targets. Once occupied, that track's leftmost building can no longer slide (Chris, same thread).
  - On IT5, "slam all of 1 track's remaining buildings flush left" (Chris).
  - Set-aside buildings stay set aside for the rest of the game. They can never be placed later, but setting one aside does reveal the income space beneath (Mike Young, BGG 3049206; Joe Aubrey, F&F product page comment 166882, 2026-08-06).
  - Text history: a Feb 2023 preview quote (BGG 3035872) read "income turns 2-3" and "(not next in your capital city)". The r4 print file reads "2-4" and "(not in your capital city)". Jamey calls the Faefolk turn range the only F&F civ misprint, so use **2-4**. **[UNVERIFIED]**: exact first-printing wording.
  - Set-aside buildings **do** count when scoring that building type (Mike Young, BGG 3092453 and 3050511).
  - Designers agree that all income buildings outside the grid (set aside, beside the mat, etc.) form one zone that counts for "per building of this type", and the count is effectively 5 minus the buildings still on that track [DQ 1-2]. **[UNVERIFIED]**: whether the IT5 option "1 VP per income building in your capital city" also counts set-aside buildings. BGA counts them.
  - Setting aside is not "gaining" a building. BGA reading; designers confirmed only the related Mystics part [DQ 3].
  - **Engine note**: the rulebook says moving an item off the income mat does not immediately generate income. Uncovered spaces pay on later income turns. The Artificers act at the start of the income turn, so the new layout pays that same turn.

#### Celestials — "a plot of acreage suspended in the sky"

- **Setup**: instead of 2 outposts on your starting territory, place 1 upright outpost and 1 player token (your floating capital).
- **Mid-game gain**:
  - Replace one outpost of your starting territory with a player token.
  - Mike Young [DQ 6]: you may choose which outpost there to replace, even an opponent's standing outpost. Auto-pick when the two are identical.
  - Also: "If you gain this civilization mid-game, you may discard it to draw another."
- **Start of income turns 2-4**: you may move the player token to an adjacent territory, even one that already has 2 outposts.
  - If you move, roll both conquer dice and gain **both** results (red + black).
  - This is not a conquer. Moving the token in does not take control of that territory.
  - A territory with 2 items (such as your token) may not be conquered.
- **Start of income turn 5**: lose 2 VP per landmark that hangs off the side of your capital city; gain 5 VP per landmark that does not hang off.
- **Rulings**:
  - The token alone never grants control (Joe Aubrey, BGG 3150783). If your outposts are all conquered you cannot explore or conquer from the token.
  - The move must be onto an explored territory [DQ 4].
  - The black die "?" face pays the benefit of the territory moved into [DQ 5].
  - With City in a Bottle (no landmarks in the city) you neither lose nor gain (Mike Young, BGG 3036298).

#### Elder Ones — "keep the secrets of extraordinary longevity"

- **Setup**: none.
- **Income turns 2-4**: gain either 1 resource of your choice, or 2 tapestry cards.
- **Income turn 5**: discard up to 6 tapestry cards from your hand; gain 2 resources of your choice per card discarded (max 12).
- **After income turn 5** you may keep taking advance turns as usual, but you may not play trap or other response cards, nor take further income turns. During this period:
  - Any THIS ERA tapestry on top of era 4 stays in effect for the rest of your game unless you cover it by playing another tapestry (via a "play a tapestry on top" benefit).
  - Each landmark you gain after income turn 5 scores 10 VP.
  - Your game ends when you are no longer able to take an advance turn.
- **Rulings**:
  - Keep playing as long as you can advance (Mike Young, BGG 3057126).
  - 10 VP per landmark, not once (Mike Young, BGG 3368650).
  - Track-space benefits, including "when played" effects of tapestry cards played via e.g. Military space 10, work normally (BGG 3576555, unofficial).
  - Ending is not voluntary: play until you can't. A track blocked by Theocracy, Dictatorship, etc. counts as unable [DQ 8].
  - Any era-4 tapestry card remains in effect through income turn 5 and after it [DQ 7].
  - A second civ's final-income-turn scoring (Islanders, Riverfolk) happens on income turn 5 [DQ 9].
  - Holding both Merfolk and Elder Ones: choose a Merfolk or an Elder Ones turn each turn (Mike Young, BGG 3052380).
  - The extra turns are taken **one at a time in normal turn order**, not all at once (Chris Scaffidi, F&F product page comment 59946, 2023-03-30).
  - Playing a tapestry onto the era-4 stack after IT5 (e.g. via a track space) may trigger a right-charm bonus if that charm has not triggered before. A charm never triggers twice (Jamey, product page comment 63601, 2023-10-11).
  - **[UNVERIFIED]**: which card's right charm is meant. Presumably the card that ends up immediately to its left.

#### Faefolk — "cyclically flicker among adjacent realities"

- **Setup** (or when gained mid-game): place a player token on the top spot of the 7-spot ellipse printed on the mat.
- **Start of income turns 2-5**:
  1. You may gain 1 tapestry card.
  2. Count your visible tapestry cards: those in your hand plus those visible on your income mat (covered cards don't count). Move the token clockwise that many spots.
  3. Gain the benefit shown on the token's new spot. On income turn 5 gain it **twice**.
- **Ellipse**, clockwise from the top (7 spots; spot 1 is the start):

| Spot | Benefit (all items on the spot) |
|---|---|
| 1 (top, start) | Score 1 VP per tapestry card (hand + mat) |
| 2 | Gain 1 tapestry card + 1 territory tile + invent 1 tech card + score 1 VP per income building (all types summed [DQ 10]) |
| 3 | Gain 1 food + score 1 VP per territory tile in your supply |
| 4 | Gain 1 coin + score 1 VP per tech card |
| 5 | Gain 1 culture + score 1 VP per territory you control |
| 6 | Gain 1 worker + **score 1 VP per landmark** (tower-in-laurel icon; BGA mislabels this as capital-city scoring) |
| 7 | Gain 1 space tile + explore with a space tile |

- **Rulings**:
  - IT2-5 only (Mike Young, BGG 3053736; Jamey, BGG 3176905 article 43181818, 2023-10-26, calling this the one F&F civ-mat misprint; Chris Scaffidi via Facebook, relayed in BGG 3048419).
  - "Visible" includes the hand and excludes covered cards (Chris; Mike).
  - Movement wraps around: count mod 7.
  - **[UNVERIFIED]**: whether the card just gained in step 1 counts. It is in hand, so presumably yes; BGA counts it.

#### Genies — "grant wishes, though not altruistically"

- **Setup** (or when gained mid-game): collect 1 player token from each opponent.
- **Income turns 2-4**:
  - Randomly draw one of those tokens to choose an opponent, then return it to your collection (it can be drawn again).
  - That opponent chooses and scores 1 circled benefit.
  - You score the same circled benefit and also gain one squared benefit adjacent to the chosen circle. Use your 2 benefits in either order.
- **Start of income turn 5**: gain 2 different circled benefits of your choice.
- **Ring** (8 positions, clockwise from top; each circle sits between two squares):
  1. square: roll the science die, may advance without benefit/bonus;
  2. circle: score 1 VP per landmark (tower-in-laurel icon);
  3. square: invent 1 tech card;
  4. circle: score 1 VP per territory tile in supply;
  5. square: gain 1 culture;
  6. circle: score 1 VP per territory you control;
  7. square: explore (with a territory tile);
  8. circle: score 1 VP per tech card.
- **Adjacency**:

| Circle | Adjacent squares |
|---|---|
| 1 VP per landmark | research-no-benefit, invent |
| 1 VP per territory tile | invent, culture |
| 1 VP per territory controlled | culture, explore |
| 1 VP per tech card | explore, research-no-benefit |

- **Rulings**:
  - Removed in solo. Jamey once said "Genies are fine" in solo (2024-03-29) while reading the defective Automa rulebook r5-1. The FAQ, Morten Pedersen and Automa rulebook r6 all say remove them.
  - A finished opponent's token can still be drawn, but that player gains nothing [DQ 11]. BGA lets the Genies player choose in their place; this is the implementer's ruling, not the designers'.
  - Jamey's worked example (product page comment 67706, 2024-03-08): you randomly draw the blue token. Blue chooses and scores "1 VP per tech card". You also score 1 VP per tech card and gain **one** adjacent square, e.g. explore **or** the science-die advance.

#### Illuminati — "profit from the lowest and highest urges of humanity"

- **Setup** (or when gained mid-game):
  - Draw 3 tapestry cards, keep 1, discard 2.
  - Then place all 3 dice (the 2 conquer dice + the science die) on this mat.
  - If your group uses a separate set of dice per player, discard this civilization and draw another. Mike Young: technically mandated, though a friendly group may play with one shared set (BGG 3095955).
- **When an opponent takes the conquer dice from your mat**: you gain both rolled benefits.
- **When an opponent takes the science die from your mat**: you may advance on the rolled track, without benefit or bonus.
- **Your own rolls**: if you take a die from your mat to roll it, put it back after rolling.
- **Opponents' rolls**:
  - If an opponent rolls a die, it stays off your mat until your next income turn.
  - If an opponent rerolls a die, you gain only from the first roll after they took it.
- **Start of income turns 2-5**: gain 6 VP per die still on this mat (max 18). Then put all 3 dice back on the mat.
- **Rulings**:
  - Each die taken pays its own benefit, ignoring rerolls; multiple dice taken at once each pay. Joe Aubrey via the Stonemaier Discord, relayed in BGG 3245586 (semi-official).
  - This covers Treasure Hunters rolling the red die alone.
  - It also covers science-die rolls caused by tapestry cards such as Chimera (Mike Young, BGG 3058558).
  - The black "?" face pays the conquered territory's benefit to the Illuminati [DQ 12].
  - The Illuminati gain resolves before the roller uses the roll, so the Illuminati win landmark races [DQ 13].
  - After the Illuminati player's game ends: Mike says the VP part still pays as a passive ability; Chris says nothing. Designers disagree [DQ 14]; BGA uses Mike's reading.
  - Gained during the civ step of IT2-5: score 18 VP that same turn [DQ 15].
  - The Illuminati gain **both** rolled conquer benefits even though the conquering opponent gains only one. The Dutch translation saying "both players" is wrong (Jamey, product page comment 66127, 2024-01-15).
  - Dice taken by an opponent stay off the mat until your next income turn, even if you conquer in the meantime (Jamey, comment 60106, 2023-04-08).
  - Removed in solo.

#### Merfolk — "build a hidden world underwater"

- **Setup**: none.
- **Start of income turns 2-4**:
  - Gain 1 tapestry card.
  - Then place all but 2 of the tapestry cards in your hand under this mat ("submerged").
  - During eras 2-4 you may look at submerged cards but may not play, spend or score them.
  - Continue to use and gain cards in hand as usual.
- **Start of income turn 5**: gain 1 tapestry card and return all submerged cards to your hand.
- **End of income turn 5**: keep up to 5 tapestry cards; discard the rest.
- **After income turn 5** you only use tapestry cards (no income or advance turns). Each turn, do one:
  - **(a)** discard any number of tapestry cards from hand for 5 VP each; or
  - **(b)** play 1 tapestry card onto the era-4 stack for its WHEN PLAYED ability.
    - Any "[5]" reserved-item effect it triggers occurs immediately.
    - Gain any left-hand charm bonus as usual.
- If attacked, you may play response cards from your hand. Your game ends when you have no tapestry cards left.
- **Rulings**:
  - Cards gained during this phase may be used (BGG 3053944, unofficial; mat says you may gain cards into hand).
  - At least one card must be discarded in option (a); no pass [DQ 16].
  - Only cards with a WHEN PLAYED ability may be played in (b). Traps do have a WHEN PLAYED ability [DQ 17].
  - The era-4 card stays in effect as for the Elder Ones (BGA reading of [DQ 7]).
  - After IT5, Merfolk keep taking their turns in normal turn order even if every other player has finished (Jamey, product page comment 86217, 2024-09-07).

#### Psionics — "sample nearby realities and choose the one they prefer"

- **Always on**: whenever you gain something random, generate one extra option and pick 1, discarding the other. Mike Young: "draw 1 extra of whatever random thing you draw and discard one; this also applies to rolls" (BGG 3032237, 2023-02-24).
  - Whenever you roll a die, roll it twice and choose 1 of the 2 values.
  - Whenever you gain a random tech card / tapestry card / masterpiece card / territory tile / space tile / civilization from its deck or supply, draw two, keep one, and shuffle the other back.
    - The third icon matches the A&A "gain 1 masterpiece card" icon. BGA's transcription calls it "landmark card". **[UNVERIFIED]**: designer intent.
  - Applies only to random gains, not to face-up choices nor to merely revealing.
- **Stacking**: the effect adds to (does not multiply) other extra-option effects. With Empiricism you roll 3 times, not 4.
  - Draw-3-keep-1 becomes draw 4 keep 1 [DQ 18].
  - Two separate random draws (e.g. two face-down tech cards) are each sampled [DQ 18].
  - Rulebook FAQ p29-30, more specific:
    - **Scouting**: draw 2 keep 1, twice; shuffle leftovers back.
    - **Age of Sail**: draw 2 keep 1, three times. Drawing 6, keeping 3 and returning 3 is acceptable to speed play.
  - Only deck/supply draws count, not random cards from a hand or discard [DQ 19].
  - With Traders, the unclaimed conquer die shows its last physical roll [DQ 20].
- **Start of income turns 2-5**:

| Income turn | Gain |
|---|---|
| 2 | 1 territory tile |
| 3 | 1 tapestry card |
| 4 | invent 1 tech card |
| 5 | research (roll science die, may advance with benefit and may pay for bonus) |

- **Optional variants**: Psionics draw 3 keep 1 in the "draw 2 tapestries on IT1" and "draw 2 civilizations" variants.

#### Weefolk — "live unnoticed in woods and towns"

- **Start of income turns 2-5, or when gained mid-game**:
  - Give one of your player tokens to an opponent of your choice.
  - They choose a plot in their capital city for it. If their city is full, they replace a building with your token and that building is set aside.
  - That plot is now filled and counts toward the opponent's districts, rows and columns under their capital's rules.
- **Income turns 2-4**: you may spend 1 territory tile to gain 1 income building of your choice, once per income turn.
- **Income turn 5**: look at each row and each column containing one of your tokens in opponents' cities. Score 1 VP per income building or landmark anywhere in those rows and columns. A building is scored more than once when it is in more than one token's row, or in a token's row and also its column.
- **Rulings**:
  - Scoring is per token per line. A multi-plot landmark counts once per line its footprint touches. The IT5 token is placed before scoring [DQ 21].
  - In a full city only an income building or a 1x1 landmark may be replaced [DQ 22]. A reprint would say "an income building".
  - Tokens may be given to finished players [DQ 23]. Jamey: allowed, but "in the spirit of the ability, if there is an active opponent, you should give the token to them" (Rules FAQ page comment 62639, 2023-09-05).
  - Placing the token is not "placing a building": no Terraforming VP, and no impassable plots [DQ 24].
  - Removed in solo.

#### Werefolk — "occasionally regress in a feral pursuit of their goals"

- **Setup**: none. No space tile at setup (official F&F FAQ, quoted by Jamey in BGG 3057193).
- **Start of income turns 2-5**:
  1. If there is a space tile on this mat, discard it.
  2. Gain a random space tile and flip it like a coin onto a clear area.
     - **Lands face-up**: you may regress on 1 track (no benefit, no bonus).
       - If you regressed, explore with that space tile by placing it onto this mat.
       - If you did not regress, gain 4 VP.
     - **Lands face-down**: advance on 1 track and gain the benefit and the bonus for free.
  3. If you did not explore with it, keep the tile in your supply for future exploration like any other space tile.
- **Rulings**:
  - You cannot "regress" a token already on a track's starting space. You explore only if a token actually moved back (FAQ p29).
  - **Engine notes**:
    - Exploring a space tile normally means placing it next to your income mat and gaining its benefit. Here it goes on the civ mat and is discarded at the next income turn.
    - You gain its benefit when you explore it.
    - The tile stays on the mat only if you explored it, and it is discarded at the next income turn. Its ongoing effects (e.g. "whenever you advance on X, gain 5 VP") therefore last one era (Joe Aubrey, product page comment 108641, 2024-12-23).
- Psionics: a coin flip is not a die roll (BGA reading).

#### FF.4.2 Response-card procedure (F&F wording)

- **Header** printed on Ghost Ship, Gremlins, The Kraken, League of the Charmed and Robotic Decoys: "You may discard this when an opponent tries to conquer your territory. If you do, topple their outpost and gain 1 of these charm bonuses."
  - "1 of these" = choose the left OR the right charm.
  - The attacker keeps their conquer-dice benefit and is denied the middle-island achievement, as with a trap (rulebook p12).
- **As a tapestry**:
  - Ghost Ship, Gremlins and The Kraken: "If played as a tapestry, gain 7 VP."
  - League of the Charmed: "If played as a tapestry, gain 1 culture and 2 VP."
  - Robotic Decoys: "If played as a tapestry, THIS ERA: After you or your neighbor advances on the science track, you may spend 1 coin. If you do so, then gain 1 worker and 5 VP."
- **[UNVERIFIED]**: whether the L/R charms of these five also trigger as normal charms when played as a tapestry. Nothing excludes it.
- Not playable as responses after your income turn 5, except by the Merfolk.
- **vs the Automa**: when you attack the Automa and it draws one of these from its face-down tapestry cards, it works like a trap (Automa rulebook p8).

#### FF.4.3 Per-card effects (paraphrased; numbers exact)

1. **Alien Disclosure**
   - Gain 1 space tile.
   - Either spend 5 VP to explore it now (if you have the VP to spend), or keep it in your supply to explore later like any space tile.
   - (A forum user thought the preview text was truncated. The printed text reads as above.)
2. **Astral Expeditions** (THIS ERA)
   - If you pay for an advancement bonus, you may forgo that bonus and instead gain a space tile or explore a space tile.
   - **[UNVERIFIED]**: whether "explore a space tile" means drawing a new tile, or exploring one already in your supply.
3. **Chimera-Citizen Rights**
   - Roll the science die, then gain the benefit for the face rolled:
     - military or exploration → explore with a territory tile **and** gain 1 culture;
     - science or technology → upgrade 1 tech card;
     - arts (A&A d20 only) → gain 1 inspiration tile.
   - You do **not** advance on the rolled track (no die-advance icon; community consensus, BGG 3695206).

### FF.6 Advanced capital city mats (6)

#### 2.0 Rules common to all six

- **Setup (RB p4, step A):**
  - Draw a basic mat as usual: 4-5 players draw 1; 1-3 players draw a random pair.
  - With A&A or F&F, also draw a random advanced mat. Keep 1 mat.
  - Do not mix basic and advanced mats when making the pairs.
  - "We recommend that new players keep a basic capital city mat."
- **Starting territory:** your 2 outposts go on the map territory matching the **basic** mat number(s) you drew, even if you keep the advanced mat.
  - The house-icon number in each F&F mat header is a mat ID. The map only has territories 1-6, so these numbers are not territory numbers.
  - Mat IDs read from TOL frames (`hdrs.png`): **Quagmire 13, City in a Bottle 14, Veil 15, Savanna 16, Polar 17, Netherworld 18.**
  - A&A advanced mats are presumably 7-12 **[UNVERIFIED]**.
- **Solo:** the human always starts on the territory labelled "2/4", whichever mat they use (ARB6 p2).
- **Impassable plots:**
  - You cannot build on them, but they count toward completing districts, rows and columns (RB p14).
  - Landmarks cannot be placed on impassable plots (JS, SMG FAQ comment c61238).
  - Unicorns (Savanna) and will-o'-the-wisps (Quagmire) count as impassable terrain for cards and civs that refer to impassable terrain, such as the Riverfolk (MY, thread 3144801).
- **Rows and columns** still score 1 VP each when you score your city.
  - City in a Bottle has 6 rows and 6 columns, so 12 lines.
  - Every other F&F mat has 9 rows and 9 columns, so 18 lines.
- **Hanging off the grid:**
  - RB p14: landmarks may hang off the grid "except in the case of certain advanced city mats that disallow hanging off the grid".
  - None of the 6 F&F mats has text forbidding hang-off. City in a Bottle forbids placing landmarks in the city at all.
  - Netherworld ignores "adjacency off the map" for its paths.
- **Impassable count:** every F&F 9×9 mat has exactly **22 impassable plots** (59 open). City in a Bottle has **17 of 36** (19 open), matching the "19 spaces" in thread 3065940. This was checked by script.

Grid legend used below:
- `.` open plot
- `X` impassable (red dot)
- `U` unicorn (impassable)
- `W` will-o'-the-wisp (impassable)
- `I` imp plot (open, must be covered)
- `C` city centre (open, must be covered)
- `r` reed plot (open)

#### 3.1 Income mat overlays ("[5]" card), 5 copies, FF

- **Setup (RB p4, step C):** "Place an income mat overlay over the right edge of your income mat, tucking the right side of the overlay beneath your capital city mat."
- **Printed face** (RB p2 component image; RB p7 example; MILL frame `millf/z_ovl.jpg`; PH 7454633). It is a card. Held landscape:
  - The half under the city has a grey map-texture background and the text **"TUCK RIGHT HALF BENEATH CITY"**. That is its only text.
  - The visible half carries on the income mat's landscape art band (yellow sky, green fields, swirl border).
  - Centred in the band is a **"5" icon box (green top half, yellow bottom half)**. RB p16 defines this icon: "Income turn 5 (e.g., use reserved items, immediately before the upgrade step)".
  - Below the band is an ornamental frame with a circled "(5)". It continues the circled "(3)" and "(4)" era frames on the income mat.
  - No other icons, numbers or rules text.
  - The back is not shown in any source **[UNVERIFIED: back side / single- or double-sided]**.
- **Function (RB p7):**
  - Some tapestry cards with WHEN PLAYED abilities place items (buildings, cards, tiles) "at the right edge of your income mat", i.e. on the overlay. These items are reserved.
  - Reserved items are used during income turn 5, at the point where you would ordinarily play a tapestry card (immediately before the upgrade step).
  - A reserved income building has already left the income mat track, so its income improvement applies right away. It goes into the city only on income turn 5.
  - Reserved items cannot be used earlier (e.g. Tunnels cannot take a reserved farm). They are still used if the reserving tapestry is later covered.
  - Reserved cards and tiles go face up.
  - Playing a tapestry after era 4 that reserves an item means you use the item immediately (RB p15).
- JS chose an overlay instead of reprinting the income mats, for cost reasons (thread 3034878). The design diary calls it "the [5] cardmat".

    1. Reshuffle the kaiju deck if it is empty.
    2. Return both blockers to beside the board.
    3. Draw a card. Move a blocker to the track of the bottommost non-S track icon on it.

    - You have no outposts on the map (lose).

#### FF.8.5 Compatibility summary

### 4.1 Revisions to P&P and A&A civs

Source: the official revision Google Sheet (`bit.ly/3URyOTn`, column "Pre-Typesetting Revisions") and Jamey's posts. | Civ | Revision (vs. original printing) |
|---|---|
| Advisors (heavily revised) | Start with 3 tapestry cards. At the beginning of your income turns **1-4**, place a player token on a tapestry slot of an opponent's income mat that doesn't already hold your token. When that opponent would play a tapestry card on a slot with your token, they return the token and reveal the card they want to play: if it is a WHEN PLAYED card and you agree, they play it and then you also gain its WHEN PLAYED benefit; if you disagree or it's another type, you pick a WHEN PLAYED card from your hand that they may play instead — if they play it you gain its benefit after them; if you have no WHEN PLAYED card, or they refuse and play their own card, you gain any 1 resource + 1 tech card + 1 tapestry card + 1 territory tile. | Islanders | Ability works on income turns **1-5** (instead of 2-5). |
| Recyclers | Can now gain a tech card from the discard pile and upgrade it during their income turns **[exact wording UNVERIFIED]**. |

## 7. Cross-expansion compatibility and combination rules

| Situation | Rule |
|---|---|
| Any subset of P&P / A&A / F&F | All expansions are designed to be shuffled permanently into the base components and to work in any combination; no expansion requires another. |
| Arts track not used (A&A or F&F owned) | Don't separate components: when drawing a tapestry card, tech card or civ that refers to the arts track (arts icon), discard it and draw another. Masterpieces/inspiration tiles unusable. F&F cards that mention the arts track always pair it with a core option, so they stay in. |
| Landmark cards from P&P **and** A&A | Shuffle them together; deal players + 1 for the opening draft; remaining cards form the landmark deck. (Single expansion: deal exactly the player count.) |
| Advanced capital city mats (A&A + F&F) | Shuffle all 12 advanced mats together; never mix with basic mats. Draw 1 basic (or a 1-3p basic pair) + 1 advanced. |
| Science die | d20 whenever the arts track is in play (optionally either die per the variant); base d12 otherwise. |
| Tiebreaker / same-space / no responses after final income turn | Apply to all games (P&P / A&A rule amendments). |
| Income turn 5 tapestry plays | Since F&F (applies regardless of F&F): any tapestry played on/after income turn 5 is placed on the era-4 stack; reserved F&F items are used immediately in that case. |
| Civ adjustments | Original mats: use the F&F p.32 list. Revised mats: no adjustments. Never mix both for one civ. |
| P&P solo campaign | Not compatible with any A&A or F&F component; needs original Historians, Islanders, Mystics. |
| Automa | Use the comprehensive F&F Automa rulebook; replace base decision cards with A&A versions permanently once you own A&A; use A&A's Hucksters/Trailblazers card. Removal list combines all expansions (see F&F part). |
| Space tiles | Base 15 (with corrected misprint) + P&P 4. Ongoing P&P tiles end when the tile is discarded (Aliens, Werefolk). |
| Tapestry deck size | Base 50 + P&P 15 + A&A 20 + F&F 38 = 123 tapestry cards (the F&F component table counts "38 (+1*)" for F&F, the +1 being the corrected Migration that replaces the A&A copy). Tech cards: 33 + 11 + 12 = 56 (F&F adds a corrected Oil Paint replacing A&A's). Landmark cards: 5 + 5 = 10 (F&F replaces 3 A&A cards). Masterpieces 20. |
| Civ count | Base 16 + P&P 10 + A&A 5 + F&F 10 = **41** civilizations. |
| Landmark miniatures | Base 18 + P&P 7 + A&A 8 = 33 (F&F adds none; City in a Bottle converts landmarks to 6 VP). |

### Fantasies & Futures (rulings: contradictions and open points)

1. **Genies in solo.** The FF-FAQ, MMP (2023, 2024, 2026) and ARB r6 say remove them. JS (2024-03-29) said "Genies are fine", reading the defective r5-1. **Resolution:** remove.
2. **Historians vs Heralds adjustment.** JS (2023-02-24, 2023-03-19) said it is for the Heralds and there is no Historians adjustment. JS (2023-05-30) answered as if it applied to the Historians. RB r10-1 relabels it HERALDS. **Resolution:** Heralds.
3. **Do set-aside or unplaced buildings score?**
   - MY: Artificers' set-aside buildings and City in a Bottle's set-aside landmarks count for per-type and per-landmark icons; landmark icons count "anywhere, even if set aside or on a card".
   - JS (2025): unplaced buildings don't score "unless specifically indicated by a card or civ".
   - JA (2023): reserved buildings aren't "in your capital city" before income turn 5.
   - **Workable rule:** "per X" icons count all of yours; effects worded "in your capital city" count only placed pieces.
4. **Is every F&F tapestry card charmed?** MY says yes (2024). The design diary and Charmers wording suggest only a subset. Verify against the card data.
5. **Dictatorship (or any "until your next turn" effect) played at the end of a player's game.** MY is unsure; JS (Heralds case) says it stops at that player's next, end-of-game turn. Not ruled precisely for Printing Press on income turn 5.
6. **RB p15 "tapestry after era 4" asymmetry.** It allows covering at the start of income turn 5 but not at the start of turns 2-4. MY acknowledged the gap is unintended and allows a house rule.
7. **Celestials + City in a Bottle.** MY rules 0 VP either way. The community argues the rules as written give +5 per landmark. Follow MY.
8. **Number of printings.** JS (2023-03) said "only printing", but the expansion was later restocked and JS (2025) says typos get fixed in the next printing. RB r10-1 is the corrected text.
9. **Open questions with no official answer:**
   - A Time Travelers benefit before returning to the track start.
   - Whether off-mat landmarks count for Quagmire "+3".
   - The German Colonialism FAQ mismatch.
   - Alien Disclosure's possibly truncated preview text.
   - Which card's right charm the Elder Ones trigger when playing onto the era-4 stack (CH-4).
